# ISEFS website, member portal and payments

Three releases that can go live independently. Bank or payment verification never blocks the public website.

## Phase 1 — Public website (build now; review by 9 October, launch by 16 October 2026)

- Brand system derived from the approved logo: deep navy, restrained teal, white, very pale grey; editorial typography; generous whitespace; restrained motion with reduced-motion support; visible focus states.
- Header with the supplied logo intact, navigation to About, Education & Science, Membership, Leadership and Contact, and a prominent "Register your interest" button. Responsive mobile menu and shared footer.
- Pages: Home, About, Education & Science (six clearly labelled areas), Membership, Leadership, Contact.
- Finished British-English copy. ISEFS described as being established. Developing programmes labelled as such. The Barcelona course (15–17 October 2026) is referenced with a link to endofacemasters.com, with the Society announcement presented as planned for 16 October and easy to update.
- Leadership presented typographically: Dr. Marc Mani, Dr. Gad Renert, Dr. Artur Díaz Carandell as scientific founders; Samuel Blum as Managing Director. No invented portraits or credentials.
- Interest registration and contact enquiry forms storing submissions privately in the project backend, with validation, spam protection, duplicate prevention and accessible loading, success and error states. Interest registration works with no bank account, payment provider or paid membership involved.
- Draft Privacy and Legal pages prepared for owner approval, kept out of the main navigation.
- Page titles, descriptions, social-sharing details, descriptive alt text, optimised images, and a square favicon from the logo.
- Verification pass over every route, CTA and form state on mobile and desktop.

## Phase 2 — Member portal (November 2026)

- Secure accounts, application submission and individual application status.
- Formal applications open only once admission rules and terms are approved; until then the site continues with interest registration.
- Staff management area to review applications, record Board admission decisions, manage access permissions, track membership and billing status separately, and export the membership register.
- Account creation or application never confers membership or access to member-only resources.
- Portal remains fully usable while payment collection is disabled.

## Phase 3 — Payments (November target, only when confirmed ready)

- Protected server-side payments setting, disabled by default. While disabled, no checkout creation, payment links, card collection, automatic debits or reminders are possible — not merely hidden buttons.
- Billing states: no payment due, collection deferred, payment requested, paid, refunded. Deferred is never paid or overdue; overdue only after an approved due date passes.
- Before any payment request, the office confirms category, fee, currency, contribution period and due date.
- Payments confirmed only through verified provider events or authorised reconciliation, with duplicate protection. Test activity isolated from live records.
- Enabling collection later preserves existing applications and decisions, and charges nobody automatically.

## Handover

A short owner checklist after each phase: what is ready, what remains, and which external confirmations are needed (contact details, privacy notice, domain, admission rules, fees, bank account, provider verification).
